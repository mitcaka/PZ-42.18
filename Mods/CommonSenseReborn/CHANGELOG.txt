[h3]New Features[/h3]
[list]
[*] Added [b]Gear Sling[/b]: a new [i]csr:gearsling[/i] body location lets players wear a sling-style or shoulder bag (duffel bags, satchels, military bags, chest rigs, etc.) alongside their primary backpack. Fanny packs get dedicated [i]csr:fannypackfront[/i] and [i]csr:fannypackback[/i] locations so they can coexist with both a backpack and a gear sling simultaneously. The legacy [i]csr:fannypack[/i] location is remapped to the front slot for save compatibility. Sandbox: [i]EnableGearSling[/i] (default on).
[*] Added [b]Rope Tow[/b]: towing a dead vehicle (no engine power and no battery charge) now requires a rope, chain, heavy chain, or heavy chain hook from the driver's inventory. The item is consumed on attach and returned to the detacher's inventory when the tow is released. Sandbox: [i]EnableRopeTow[/i] (default on).
[*] Added [b]Corpse Trunk[/b]: right-clicking a nearby corpse shows [i]Put Corpse in Trunk[/i] when a vehicle trunk, truck bed, or cargo container is accessible within 2 tiles. Supports standard trunk, truckbed, trailer trunk, and cargo part IDs; uses keyword matching for modded vehicle variants. Sandbox: [i]EnableCorpseTrunk[/i] (default on).
[*] Added [b]Stair Vault Guard[/b]: automatic vaulting is suppressed only when the player is beside stairs and a nearby hoppable railing, preventing accidental stairwell falls. Normal flat-ground fence auto-vaulting and manual climb/vault interactions still work. Sandbox: [i]EnableStairVaultGuard[/i] (default on).
[*] Added [b]Extended Battery Life[/b]: vanilla battery-powered light sources now use a sandboxed drain multiplier instead of a hardcoded balance patch. Flashlights, angle-head flashlights, penlights, and crafted electric lanterns last 100x longer by default, tunable with [i]BatteryLifeMultiplier[/i] or disabled with [i]EnableExtendedBatteryLife[/i].
[*] Added [b]Weaponized Brick[/b]: a CSR-owned improvised small-blunt weapon using the permissioned brick model/texture assets, craftable from a vanilla clay brick plus cloth strips and seeded into concrete/tool loot tables.
[*] Added [b]Signal Tools[/b]: CSR-owned glow sticks, hand flares, a signal pistol, and signal flare rounds with renamed CSR assets. Glow sticks can be crafted, looted, and thrown to leave temporary colored light; hand flares ignite from inventory; loaded signal pistols can fire a signal round at a target ground tile. The full set is seeded into camping, survival, electronics, gun, police, military, and security loot pools and declares proper item types/categories for the admin item list.
[*] Expanded [b]Dismantle All Watches[/b] into [b]Dismantle Small Electronics[/b]: the batch screwdriver action now accepts watches, clocks, CD players, hair dryers, hair irons, headphones, earbuds, cordless phones, remotes, pagers, speakers, amplifiers, video games, and home alarms for electronics scrap and Electrical XP.
[*] Added [b]Speed Reload[/b]: magazine-fed firearms can rapidly dump the currently inserted magazine to the ground and insert the best loaded spare magazine through a new firearm radial slice, quick Reload-key double-tap, optional keybind, or by pressing reload again while ejection is underway.
[*] Added [b]Solar Never Faded Bridge[/b]: a soft zero-dependency compatibility bridge for Solar Never Faded's optional [i]SolarNeverFadedCSRBridge[/i] wiring API. Exposes combined CSR wiring reach (the greater of Power Bar max wires and Power Line max length) to SNF's provider registry. Activates automatically when SNF is present.
[*] Added [b]Faiths & Traditions Bridge[/b]: a soft zero-dependency compatibility bridge for Faiths & Traditions' optional [i]FTRBridge[/i] / [i]FTR.Bridge[/i] survival modifier API. The Antibodies system can now receive faith-based survival modifiers when F&T is loaded, without any hard mod dependency.
[/list]

[h2]v1.8.33 -- May 5, 2026 (feature release)[/h2]

[h3]New Features[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Power Bar Cable Extension[/b] -- right-click a Power Bar in your inventory while standing on a powered tile to plug it in. Each Electric Wire invested extends its powered zone by 1 tile (Manhattan distance, default cap 12, sandbox-tunable up to 32). Right-click a placed Power Bar in the world to add wire (+1 tile) or unplug (recovers the bar plus a configurable percentage of invested wires). The zone propagates to anything that uses the standard [i]haveElectricity[/i] check -- placed radios, lamps, heaters, etc. -- and optionally extends generator coverage too. Server-authoritative registry, full-sync on join, ModData-flat persistence so it survives world reload. New sandbox toggles: [i]EnablePowerBarCable[/i], [i]PowerBarMaxCableLength[/i], [i]PowerBarCableReturnPct[/i], [i]PowerBarExtendsGenerator[/i].
[*] [b]Plug-In Placed Radios[/b] -- baseline QoL on top of the cable system: any placed radio sitting on a powered tile (grid, generator, or Power Bar zone) automatically runs on mains power instead of draining its battery. Picking it up or moving it off the powered tile flips it back to battery. No interaction needed; entirely opt-out via [i]EnableRadioPlugIn[/i] (default on).
[*] [b]EV Vehicle Conversion[/b] -- right-click any vehicle and pick [i]Convert to Electric...[/i]. The conversion panel lists required parts (defaults: 4 Car Batteries, 8 Electric Wires, 2 Duct Tape, 1 Car Battery Charger -- all sandbox-tunable). Once converted, the vehicle runs on a charge meter instead of fuel: the fuel needle is force-clamped on every tick so vanilla engine logic keeps running, charge drains based on speed and throttle, and the dashboard fuel arrows tint blue to show EV state. Reverting the conversion returns half the batteries, half the wires, and the charger. Vanilla refuel / siphon menu entries are auto-suppressed on EV vehicles to avoid confusion. Root cause: [b]CSR_HotbarTransferAttach.lua[/b] overrode [b]ISHotbar:attachItem[/b] to "handle" the container case, but the override skipped [b]self:setAttachAnim(item, slotDef)[/b] -- the vanilla call that sets the [b]AttachAnim[/b] character variable to the slot's animset (belt / back / small_belt / etc.). Without it, the engine fell back to a default clip (the surrender pose) and the attach completed incorrectly. Vanilla [b]ISHotbar:attachItem[/b] in B42 already handles container -> hotbar attaches correctly via [b]ISInventoryPaneContextMenu.transferIfNeeded()[/b]; CSR's override was a redundant duplicate of vanilla logic minus the animation setup. The override has been removed so vanilla runs unmodified.
[/list]

[h2]v1.6.5 -- April 24, 2026[/h2]

[h3]New Features[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Faction Member Limit (MP):[/b] Faction owners can no longer invite players once the faction reaches the configured cap. The Add Player button is disabled with a count indicator (e.g. "Full (8/8)") when at the limit; attempting to invite via the add-player dialog shows an error modal instead. Default cap: 8 members (including the owner). Configurable in sandbox: CSR: Server & MP → Max faction members (2–32). Disable entirely with Enable Faction Member Limit toggle. Enforcement is UI-side on the faction owner's client, consistent with the vanilla faction system (which is fully client-authoritative).
[*] [b]Admin Faction Monitor (MP):[/b] Server admins can right-click anywhere in the world to open the [b]CSR Faction Monitor[/b] panel. The panel lists every active faction with member count vs. cap, highlights factions at the limit in red, and allows admins to kick any non-owner member with a confirmation dialog. The panel auto-reads the current sandbox cap each refresh, so cap changes are reflected immediately without relogging.
[*] [b]Faction Safehouse Claiming (MP):[/b] Faction owners can right-click any unclaimed building to claim it as a faction safehouse (separate from the owner's personal safehouse allowance). Up to [i]Max Faction Safehouses[/i] safehouses can be claimed per faction (default: 2, configurable 1–5). Other faction members automatically see the building labeled in the context menu. The faction owner or a server admin can release a faction safehouse at any time. Feature can be disabled with the [b]Enable Faction Safehouse Claiming[/b] sandbox toggle.
[*] [b]Survivor's Ledger:[/b] Optional compact HUD panel showing 6 live survival statistics: days survived, total zombie kills, distance traveled, character weight, session kills, and average kills per day. Stats are persisted across saves. Draggable — position saved per-character. Default ON; toggle with Numpad 4 or the [b]LD[/b] button in the Utility HUD. Keybind rebindable via Mod Options. Crisp color-coded stat badges drawn natively by the UI engine (never degrades at any resolution). Toggle in sandbox under Interface & QoL.
[*] [b]Aim-Down-Sights Ammo Counter:[/b] A floating ammo pill now appears near your cursor when aiming any firearm. Fully independent from the hotbar pill strip and the top-left weapon HUD counter — toggle each separately in sandbox: CSR: Gameplay → Enable Aim-Down-Sights Ammo Counter.
[*] [b]Gear Sling:[/b] Adds a dedicated second equip slot for shoulder bags, satchels, chest rigs, duffel bags, and other crossbody carriers. Carry a sling bag alongside your main backpack with no weight penalty. Toggle in sandbox: CSR: Gameplay → Enable Gear Sling.
[*] [b]Rally Point Beacon (MP):[/b] Right-click the world map to set a personal rally point. Share it with all online survivors to broadcast an amber waypoint pin and a dashed route line visible on both the world map and minimap. The waypoint auto-clears when you arrive within 15 tiles. Toggle in sandbox: CSR: Server & MP → Enable Rally Point Beacon.
[*] [b]Survivor Bond (MP):[/b] Players who remain near each other for an extended time accumulate a bond. Once the threshold is met, Stress, Boredom, Fatigue, and Unhappiness gradually decrease as long as the proximity is maintained. Bond strength scales up with continued closeness. All parameters (radius, threshold, which stats to reduce) are configurable in sandbox: CSR: Server & MP.
[/list]

[h3]Improvements[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Zombie Density Overlay — improved accuracy:[/b] Scan radius increased from 1 to 2 cells (now shows a 5×5 grid / 500×500 tile area instead of 3×3 / 300×300), and the refresh rate doubled (every ~5 s instead of ~10 s). The server already walks all loaded objects in a single pass, so the wider radius adds no extra cost — it just bins more of those already-found zombies into visible cells.
[*] [b]Tow Assist — smoother feel:[/b] Replaced the previous "flat-top" speed curve (full force at ≤20 km/h, linear taper to zero at 60 km/h) with a continuously-decreasing taper that begins at 5 km/h and reaches zero at 55 km/h. The old flat top caused a noticeable lurch at low speeds; the new curve delivers the same top-end assist but ramps up progressively from the moment the vehicle starts moving. Also added the speed taper to the MP server-side path, which was previously missing it (MP players received full assist at any speed).
[*] [b]In-game guide updated[/b] — Added entries for 7 features added since the last guide update (v1.4.8): Notice Board, Video Insert/Eject, ADS Ammo Counter, Gear Sling, Rally Point Beacon, Survivor Bond, and Survivor's Ledger. Added new Multiplayer section.
[*] [b]Personal Settings panel updated[/b] — ADS Ammo Counter and Survivor's Ledger now appear as toggles in the S-panel alongside the existing 13 per-player preference overrides.
[/list]

[h3]Performance Improvements[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Utility HUD dirty-flag cache[/b] — Status lines and string measurements are now rebuilt only when state actually changes, eliminating 6+ string.format calls and MeasureStringX evaluations per frame at 60 fps.
[*] [b]Zombie Density Overlay early cull[/b] — Off-screen cells are now rejected with a fast center-point check before projecting all 4 corners, reducing wasted worldToUI math for cells outside the visible map panel.
[*] [b]Double-registration guards[/b] — Added idempotency guards to 7 event handler registrations across 5 files (OnTick, OnPlayerUpdate, OnGameStart, OnServerStarted), preventing duplicate callbacks on world reloads or module re-execution.
[/list]

[h3]Bugfixes[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Infection Resilience server crash (every game minute):[/b] [b]getInfectionMortalityTime()[/b] and [b]getInfectionLevel()[/b] no longer exist in B42 — calling them threw "Object tried to call nil" on the server every game minute whenever an infected player was online. Both calls are replaced with [b]CharacterStat.ZOMBIE_INFECTION[/b], which returns the infection progress as a 0–1 value. Behaviour is unchanged: the survival roll fires when infection reaches the configured threshold.
[*] [b]Safehouse claiming (MP) — building always visible to owner after claiming:[/b] CSR was creating safehouses by calling [b]SafeHouse.addSafeHouse()[/b] directly on the server. This triggers Java's [b]SafezoneClaimPacket[/b] with [b]playerIndex: -1[/b], which the client-consistency check rejects, so clients never received the ownership update — the safehouse existed on the server (re-claiming said "already claimed") but the owner couldn't see or manage it. Fixed: server now validates the claim (enabled, count limit, not already claimed) and sends an approval message to the client, which then calls the vanilla [b]sendSafehouseClaim()[/b] — the only path that properly carries the player's connection index.
[*] [b]Translations — field filter item names not appearing in non-English languages:[/b] The [b]ItemName.json[/b] files for DE, FR, JA, KO, PL, PT, UK, ZH, and ZH-TW used the key format [b]"ItemName_CSR_FieldRespirator"[/b] instead of the correct B42 format [b]"Base.CSR_FieldRespirator"[/b]. Wrong-format keys are silently ignored by the engine, so translated names for Field Respirator Filter and Field Gas Mask Filter were never shown. All 9 files corrected. Also removed duplicate wrong-format entries from [b]EN/UI.json[/b] (the correct keys were already in [b]EN/ItemName.json[/b]).
[*] [b]Security Key keyring placement (multi-keyring):[/b] When a player had two keyrings — one worn and one inside a bag — the permanent Security Key granted after unlocking a security/vault door could end up in the nested keyring instead of the accessible worn one. The lookup now prefers the root-inventory keyring (worn/belt) and falls back to deep search only if none is found there.
[*] [b]Nested Container Panel Gap:[/b] Vanilla resets the inventory scroll height before CSR prunes duplicate nested buttons. After pruning, the remaining buttons no longer matched the scroll area, leaving a gap at the bottom. The panel now recalculates its scroll height from the actual bottom of the last remaining button after pruning.
[*] [b]Startup crash (non-English languages):[/b] 47 translation JSON files contained a UTF-8 BOM byte sequence. Java's JSON parser requires BOM-free UTF-8 and threw "A JSONObject text must begin with '{'" at launch for all non-English players. All files corrected.
[/list]

The "WarmHands" animation name does not exist in B42 (confirmed: zero matches across all vanilla Lua files); the engine left the character in a corrupted animation state. Replaced with "WashFace", a valid B42 animation that visually matches the blow-on-hands intent.

The corrupted animation state introduced by the invalid WarmHands name persisted into subsequent ISEquipWeaponAction calls, preventing the idle pose from restoring. Fixing the WarmUp animation resolves both symptoms.
[*]Fixed security/vault doors permanently locking the player out after they close the door following a successful pry, bolt-cutter, or lockpick. Java's ToggleDoor() evaluates the forceLocked tile sprite property on every door close and unconditionally restores isLocked=true; no Lua-side modData hook fires on close, so previous attempts to re-clear the flag were never triggered. Root fix: when breaking into a forceLocked door CSR now gives the player a permanent "Security Key" item (added to keyring if available, otherwise main inventory). The key has the matching keyId Java assigned to that specific door, so vanilla's normal key-check inside ToggleDoor lets the player re-open the door with E at any time regardless of its locked state.
[*]Fixed Sleep Anywhere action silently failing to start. ISPathFindAction:pathToNearest expected a table of {x,y,z} position tables (array of locations); CSR was passing a single flat {x, y, z} table, which is not a valid location list and always produced no path. Also fixed the setOnComplete call which was using an anonymous pcall wrapper instead of the vanilla argument-passing pattern, causing the sleep callback to never fire.
[*]Fixed "Sleep On It" -- sleep now actively reduces Stress (on-edge), Panic (anxiety), Anger, Boredom, and Unhappiness every 10 in-game minutes while asleep, and applies a meaningful one-time burst reduction of all five mood stats the moment the player wakes up. Also added Endurance recovery during sleep so the Exhausted moodle clears after a full rest (vanilla does not restore sprint stamina during sleep).
[*]Fixed whiteboard text not persisting after save and reopen in SP. The panel's postCreateChildren hook fires before Java widget initialization, so all setText() calls were silently skipped; lines are now populated in prerender() on the first render pass after Java init. Text is stored as individual flat modData keys (csrWbLine1--csrWbLine6) rather than a nested Lua table, which Java's modData serializer converts to a HashMap and makes unreadable on reload.
[*]Fixed whiteboard context menu and save handler only recognising sprite variant _53. All six whiteboard variants (_50 through _55) are now detected correctly on both client and server.
[*]Fixed whiteboard marker detection using the non-existent item type "Marker". The actual B42 marker types (MarkerBlack, MarkerBlue, MarkerRed, MarkerGreen) are now checked. Applies to both the client-side save gate and the server-side validation in MP.

(2) Client-side -- CSR_DualWieldHooks now tracks the local player's last-known secondary in changeUnarmedAnimation (runs every OnPlayerUpdate). A new onAttackFinishedRestoreSecondary handler, registered before triggerLeftHandAttack on OnPlayerAttackFinished, restores the secondary from the local anchor if it was cleared within the last 10 frames (~167 ms). This ensures triggerLeftHandAttack sees ArmedMode (weapon in off-hand) rather than UnarmedMode (fists), and also covers the shove case where no left-hand attack is triggered at all.
